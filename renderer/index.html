Mohammed Sheik

CloudNex · Digital Sovereignty & Local AI

Mohammed Sheik founded CloudNex (cloudnex.co.za) with a dedication to engineering high-performance computing systems, sovereign cloud solutions, and accessible deep learning infrastructure.

CloudNex Local LLM Studio was created to eliminate the immense friction surrounding local open-weights post-training. Traditional open-source machine learning often devolves into "dependency hell" — incompatible CUDA drivers, broken pip wheels, and complex multi-step build scripts.

Zero-Pip Architecture

Bundles an embedded PyTorch distribution and compiled C++ tensor kernels inside the desktop binary for instantaneous, one-click launches.

Hardware Inclusivity

First-class optimization for AMD ROCm (RDNA 4 Navi 48 / RDNA 3), NVIDIA CUDA Ada/Ampere, Apple Metal (MPS), and multi-core CPU SIMD.

100% Private & Sovereign

No remote telemetry, no telemetry beacons, and zero cloud API dependencies. Checkpoints, datasets, and prompts stay on your physical storage.

CloudNex Local LLM Studio operates strictly on-device. The software contains zero tracking beacons, no analytics telemetry, and no hidden cloud transmissions. Your training datasets, fine-tuned weights, and private prompts remain exclusively on your local disk at all times.

Self-Contained Bundled Runtime

Zero-Pip

The desktop distribution is packaged with a frozen Python engine and pre-compiled PyTorch runtime. Users are not required to manually configure Python environments, virtualenvs, or pip dependencies. The software manages its local worker processes autonomously.

04

Hardware Thermal Headroom & Compute Safety

Compute Safety

Fine-tuning deep neural networks generates substantial computational loads on CPU and GPU silicon. The studio provides fine-grained controls for thread allocation, RAM ceilings, and VRAM reservation fractions. Users must maintain adequate chassis cooling. CloudNex is not liable for thermal throttling or system resets under sustained compute loads.
