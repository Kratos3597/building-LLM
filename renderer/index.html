CloudNex · Digital Sovereignty & Local AI

Mohammed Sheik founded CloudNex (cloudnex.co.za) with a dedication to engineering high-performance computing systems, sovereign cloud solutions, and accessible deep learning infrastructure.

CloudNex Local LLM Studio was created to eliminate the immense friction surrounding local open-weights post-training. Traditional open-source machine learning often devolves into "dependency hell" — incompatible CUDA drivers, broken pip wheels, and complex multi-step build scripts.

Zero-Pip Architecture

Bundles an embedded PyTorch distribution and compiled C++ tensor kernels inside the desktop binary for instantaneous, one-click launches.

Hardware Inclusivity

First-class optimization for AMD ROCm (RDNA 4 Navi 48 / RDNA 3), NVIDIA CUDA Ada/Ampere, Apple Metal (MPS), and multi-core CPU SIMD.

100% Private & Sovereign

No remote telemetry, no telemetry beacons, and zero cloud API dependencies. Checkpoints, datasets, and prompts stay on your physical storage.
